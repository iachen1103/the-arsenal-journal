NOVEMBER 07, 2026 · PREMIER LEAGUE

A Narrow Win, A Valuable Three Points

ARSENAL 1 – 0 HULL CITY

A frustrating afternoon turned into a memorable victory. Arsenal dominated possession but struggled to break down Hull City's organised defence. Martin Ødegaard finally found the breakthrough in the 72nd minute, reacting quickest to a rebound from Noni Madueke's shot.

It wasn't the most convincing performance, but sometimes patience is what makes the difference. Three points at the Emirates, and another chapter in our Arsenal journey.
